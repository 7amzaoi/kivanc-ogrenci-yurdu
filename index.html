Facilities

Everything included, nothing to think about

One monthly payment covers the room, the bills and the services around it — so students can put their attention where it belongs.

  • 24/7 security

    Staffed reception day and night, with keycard access at every entrance.

  • 24/7 camera recording

    Every common area and entrance is recorded around the clock and reviewed regularly.

  • Entry and exit notifications

    Every entry and exit is logged. Families who want it are told instantly on WhatsApp or Telegram.

  • Fibre Wi-Fi

    High-speed fibre in every room and lounge, built to handle exam season.

  • Hotel-style rooms

    A TV, a mini fridge and a private bathroom in every room, with brand-new furniture.

  • Breakfast included

    Breakfast every morning is part of the monthly fee, with no separate charge.

  • Hot water, always

    Hot water at any hour of the day, included in the monthly fee.

  • Laundry room

    Washers and dryers included in your rent — no coins, no queues.

  • Cleaning service

    Weekly room cleaning and daily upkeep of every shared space.

  • On-site management

    A resident manager in the building — not a phone number in another city.

  • See it before you come

    Can't visit in person? We'll walk you through the rooms live on a video call.

  • Transport at the door

    The Metrobüs, city buses and minibus routes are all a short walk from the door.

About Kıvanç

Built for students. Answerable to parents.

Since 2013 Family-run, professionally managed

Most of our residents move to Istanbul from another city, and for their families that first year is a leap of faith. We built Kıvanç around that reality: one properly staffed building, clear rules, transparent pricing, and someone reachable whenever a parent needs an answer.

We're not a large chain. The residence is run day to day by the same family that has owned it since 2013, which is why the standards hold.

  • 45 rooms in the building
  • 5 campuses within 30 minutes

What that looks like day to day

  • Safety comes first

    Controlled entry, 24/7 camera recording and staff on site at every hour — and entries and exits reported to the family as they happen.

  • We answer in your language

    Turkish and English support today, with Arabic-speaking staff for international students.

  • Transparent pricing

    One monthly figure covering bills, cleaning, laundry and meals. No surprises mid-year.

  • A real community

    Study groups, shared meals and events that make a new city feel smaller.
